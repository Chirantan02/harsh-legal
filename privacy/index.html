Privacy Policy

Harsh: Quit Doomscrolling — the app blocker that makes you confess before you scroll.
Last updated: September 19, 2026

Harsh ("the app") is built to be local-first. Your app usage monitoring, session history, contracts, confessions, and lockout data are processed and stored on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell this data. There is no account, no sign-in, and we never collect your name or email address.

What we collect

  1. Anonymous app-user ID — not linked to you. On first launch the app generates a random installation/user ID used by RevenueCat to associate purchases on this device. No account identifier, email, or contact information is collected.
  2. Usage data — not linked to you. We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. screens viewed, onboarding progress). These are not linked to your identity.
  3. Purchase history — not linked to you. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing / the App Store and managed via RevenueCat under the anonymous app-user ID above. We never see your payment details.

What we do NOT collect

  • The apps you monitor, when you open them, or how long you use them (this stays on-device).
  • The text you write in contracts, confessions, or bypasses (stored locally, never uploaded).
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

Permissions used

  • Usage access — to detect when you open an app you chose to monitor, so the app can intervene. This data never leaves your device.
  • Display over other apps — to show the contract, guillotine, and lockout screens on top of monitored apps.
  • Notifications — to deliver your scheduled reminders and intervention prompts.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — analytics.
  • RevenueCat — purchase entitlement management.

Children

Harsh is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

Requesting deletion of your data

To request deletion of the data we hold about you (your anonymous app-user ID, its RevenueCat purchase records, and associated Firebase Analytics data), email stylescape.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include the app version and approximate install date so we can locate the anonymous ID. We delete it within 30 days. Purchase records retained by Google Play, the App Store, and RevenueCat for legal/tax purposes are governed by their retention policies; anonymized analytics aggregates cannot be traced back to you. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately.
