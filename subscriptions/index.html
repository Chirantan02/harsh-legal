Subscriptions

Harsh: Quit Doomscrolling

Harsh Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. It unlocks Ghost Mode focus locks, unlimited monitored apps and the full activity history. There is no free trial and no in-app cancellation — you keep full control from your Apple account.

Plans

  • Harsh Pro Weekly — renews every week.
  • Harsh Pro Yearly — renews every year.

Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before purchase. Billing is handled by Apple; Harsh never sees your card details.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancel or manage anytime:

  • Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Harsh
  • or the App Store app → account icon → Subscriptions

After cancelling, Pro features stay active until the paid period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple through your purchase history.
