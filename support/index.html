Harsh: Quit Doomscrolling

Need help or have a question? Email stylescape.app@gmail.com — answers usually within 48 hours.

Managing your subscription

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Harsh → Cancel. Or manage it in the App Store app under Account → Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play Store app → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Harsh.

Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancelling keeps your Pro features until the paid period ends.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their purchase policies — request one through your App Store / Google Play purchase history.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device instantly. To also delete the anonymous purchase/analytics records we hold server-side, follow the deletion request process in the Privacy Policy.
